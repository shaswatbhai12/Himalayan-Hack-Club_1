About the club

Himalayan Hack Club is an official chapter of Hack Club, the global nonprofit network of teen coders. Beginners are welcome. You learn by making things, not by sitting through lectures.

Learn by building

Start from zero with websites, games and Python. Every session ends with something that runs.

Ship real projects

We put projects online so friends, family and teachers can actually use them.

Part of Hack Club

Access to Hack Club's worldwide community, programs and events for teenagers.

Hackathons

We run and join hackathons, where teams build a project in a day or a weekend.

FAQ

Quick answers before you join.

Do I need to know coding already?

No. Most members start with zero experience. We begin with the basics.

Who can join?

Students of Himalaya Public School. Check with the club leaders about other schools.

Does it cost anything?

No. Joining the club is free.

Do I need my own laptop?

It helps, but you can pair up with a teammate if you don't have one.

How do I join?

Press "Join now" and fill in the form. We will contact you with the next meeting details.

Ready to build something?

Fill in the form and come to our next meeting. No experience needed.
